## Workflow

### Weekly Tasks (Every Monday)

1. **Ranking check** — Search 5 priority keywords, note positions for 7trendzlearn vs each competitor
2. **Competitor content scan** — Check if competitors published new blog posts or landing pages
3. **Alert log** — Record any significant competitor moves (new features, pricing changes, funding)

### Monthly Tasks (First Week of Month)

1. **Full keyword sweep** — Check all 12 primary + long-tail keywords across all competitors
2. **Content gap analysis** — Identify keywords competitors rank for that we don't
3. **New comparison pages** — Create 1-2 competitor comparison/vs pages
4. **Backlink monitor** — Check new backlinks for top 3 competitors
5. **GEO check** — Run queries through ChatGPT/Perplexity to check AI citation presence

### Quarterly Tasks

1. **Full competitive teardown** — Deep profile each top 5 competitor (pricing, features, positioning, SEO)
2. **SWOT refresh** — Update SWOT for each competitor
3. **Strategy adjustment** — Revise keyword targets and content plan based on results
4. **Report to CMO** — Comprehensive ranking progress report

## SEO Battle Plan (Per Competitor)

### To Beat Learnable (learnable.co.za)
- **Their strength:** Voice + canvas AI tutor "Sage", strong branding, R149/mo pricing
- **Our angle:** More affordable, no session caps, better multi-subject support
- **Target pages:** `/ai-tutor`, `/vs/learnable`
- **Keywords:** "Learnable alternative", "better than Sage tutor", "affordable AI tutor SA"

### To Beat CapsGPT (capsgpt.com)
- **Their strength:** Full CAPS curriculum, teacher/learner/parent portals, Think-First Protocol
- **Our angle:** Auto-course-building (they don't have this), better LMS integration
- **Target pages:** `/caps-curriculum`, `/vs/capsgpt`
- **Keywords:** "CapsGPT alternative", "CAPS AI platform", "AI for South African teachers"

### To Beat StudyBru (studybru.co.za)
- **Their strength:** 25+ specialized AI tutors, multilingual 11 languages, free beta
- **Our angle:** Actual course building + tutoring (they're Q&A only), deeper learning paths
- **Target pages:** `/features`, `/vs/studybru`
- **Keywords:** "StudyBru alternative", "best AI tutoring South Africa"
