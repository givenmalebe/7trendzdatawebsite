## Key Differences

### Future Learning Wins
- **Auto-course building** — Teachers input a topic, Future Learning builds the full course with lessons, quizzes, and assessments automatically. Learnable doesn't do this.
- **Full LMS** — Not just a tutor. It's a complete Learning Management System for schools.
- **Better pricing** — More features at a lower price point.
- **Multi-student management** — Built for schools and parents managing multiple learners.

### Learnable Wins
- **Voice-first experience** — Sage is designed around natural conversation. Very polished.
- **Live canvas drawing** — Sage sketches diagrams in real-time during voice conversations.
- **Strong UX** — Beautiful, consumer-grade app experience.
- **Brand recognition** — Already trusted by students across multiple provinces.

## Who Should Choose Which?

### Choose Future Learning if:
- You're a **school or teacher** needing a full LMS
- You want **auto-generated courses** not just Q&A tutoring
- You need **multi-student progress tracking**
- Price matters — you get more for less

### Choose Learnable if:
- You just want a **conversational AI tutor** for quick help
- You love **voice-based learning** with live diagrams
- You're an **individual student** looking for exam prep
- You prefer a **polished consumer app** experience
